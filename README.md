# Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) Portfolio

Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) portfolio demonstrating vendor assessment, risk evaluation, remediation, and ongoing monitoring.

## Project Scenario

A fictional organization is onboarding a third-party SaaS vendor that will process sensitive business and customer information. This project demonstrates how a GRC professional can assess the vendor's security posture, identify and evaluate risks, document findings, recommend remediation, and support ongoing third-party risk monitoring.

## TPRM Lifecycle Demonstrated

1. Vendor Identification & Criticality
2. Due Diligence & Security Assessment
3. Risk Identification & Evaluation
4. Risk Scoring & Prioritization
5. Remediation & Risk Treatment
6. Risk Acceptance & Approval
7. Ongoing Monitoring & Reassessment
8. Vendor Offboarding
