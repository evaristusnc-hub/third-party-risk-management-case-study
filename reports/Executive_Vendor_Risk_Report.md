# Executive Vendor Risk Report

## Vendor Summary

**Vendor:** CloudServe Technologies  
**Service:** SaaS Customer Management Platform  
**Vendor Criticality:** High  
**Primary Risk ID:** TPRM-001  
**Current Risk Rating:** High

## Executive Summary

CloudServe Technologies is a high-criticality third-party SaaS vendor that processes sensitive customer information, integrates with internal systems, and supports important business operations.

During the security due diligence process, a vulnerability management control gap was identified. The vendor does not currently maintain defined remediation timelines for critical vulnerabilities.

This gap creates a High third-party risk because critical vulnerabilities may remain exploitable for extended periods, increasing the potential for unauthorized access, customer data compromise, service disruption, and regulatory or reputational impact.

## Risk Evaluation

| Risk Factor | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Likelihood | 4 - Likely |
| Impact | 4 - Major |
| Risk Score | 16 |
| Risk Rating | High |
| Treatment Decision | Mitigate |
| Risk Status | Open |

## Required Remediation

CloudServe Technologies should:

- Establish documented vulnerability remediation SLAs based on severity
- Remediate critical vulnerabilities within 15 days
- Remediate high vulnerabilities within 30 days
- Implement a documented exception process
- Establish escalation procedures for overdue vulnerabilities
- Provide monthly vulnerability remediation metrics
- Provide evidence for GRC validation before final risk closure

## Expected Residual Risk

If the remediation actions are implemented and operating effectively, the residual risk is expected to decrease from **High** to **Medium**.

## Recommendation

Proceed with onboarding only if the identified vulnerability management risk is formally accepted or the required remediation is completed within agreed timelines.

The risk should remain tracked as TPRM-001 until remediation evidence has been validated and the residual risk has been formally reassessed.

## Ongoing Monitoring

Because CloudServe Technologies is classified as a High-criticality vendor, the organization should continue periodic monitoring, including vulnerability remediation reviews, security incident oversight, compliance review, and annual third-party risk reassessment.
