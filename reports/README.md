# Vendor Risk Reporting

This section demonstrates how third-party risk assessment results are summarized and communicated to business, security, GRC, and risk stakeholders to support informed decision-making.

## Reporting Objectives

- Summarize the vendor's overall risk posture
- Communicate material third-party risks
- Highlight remediation requirements and status
- Present residual risk considerations
- Support onboarding and risk acceptance decisions
- Provide leadership with concise, decision-relevant information
