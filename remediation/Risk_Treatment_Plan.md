# Risk Treatment Plan

## Risk Reference

**Risk ID:** TPRM-001  
**Vendor:** CloudServe Technologies  
**Risk Category:** Vulnerability Management  
**Current Risk Rating:** High  
**Treatment Decision:** Mitigate

## Remediation Actions

| Action | Owner | Target Timeline | Validation Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Establish documented vulnerability remediation SLAs based on severity | Vendor Security Team | Critical: 15 days, High: 30 days, Medium: 60 days, Low: 90 days | Vulnerability Management Policy | Open |
| Implement an exception process for vulnerabilities that cannot be remediated within required timelines | Vendor Security Team | 30 days | Approved exception procedure | Open |
| Define escalation procedures for overdue critical and high vulnerabilities | Vendor Security Team | 30 days | Escalation workflow | Open |
| Provide monthly vulnerability remediation metrics | Vendor Security Team | Monthly | Vulnerability remediation report | Open |
| Validate remediation requirements before final onboarding approval | GRC / Third-Party Risk Team | Before onboarding approval | Evidence review and validation record | Open |

## Expected Residual Risk

If the remediation actions are implemented and operating effectively, the risk is expected to decrease from **High** to **Medium**.

## Validation Approach

The GRC or Third-Party Risk team should review the vendor's updated vulnerability management documentation, confirm the defined remediation timelines, inspect evidence of vulnerability tracking, and verify that exception and escalation processes are operational.

Residual risk should be formally reassessed after remediation validation.
