# Ongoing Monitoring Plan

## Vendor

**Vendor:** CloudServe Technologies  
**Vendor Criticality:** High  
**Primary Open Risk:** TPRM-001 - Vulnerability Management

## Monitoring Activities

| Monitoring Activity | Frequency | Evidence / Information Reviewed | Escalation Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Review open vulnerability remediation items | Monthly | Vulnerability remediation report | Critical or high vulnerabilities exceed approved remediation timelines |
| Review vulnerability remediation metrics | Monthly | Vulnerability metrics and aging report | Increasing overdue vulnerabilities or repeated SLA failures |
| Review security incidents | Quarterly and event-driven | Incident reports and customer notifications | Material security incident or confirmed customer data impact |
| Review security and compliance posture | Annually | SOC 2 report, security certifications, and relevant compliance documentation | Material control deficiencies or loss of required certification |
| Reassess vendor criticality and risk | Annually | Updated vendor assessment and business dependency information | Significant change in services, data access, or system integration |
| Validate TPRM-001 remediation | Before risk closure | Updated policy, remediation evidence, exception process, and escalation workflow | Required remediation is incomplete or ineffective |

## Escalation Process

Material security issues, overdue remediation, significant incidents, or changes in the vendor's risk profile should be escalated to the appropriate GRC, security, business, and risk stakeholders for review and decision-making.

## Reassessment

CloudServe Technologies should undergo a formal third-party risk reassessment at least annually because of its High criticality. Additional reassessments may be triggered by significant security incidents, material service changes, changes in data access, or major control deficiencies.

## Risk Closure

TPRM-001 should remain open until remediation evidence has been reviewed and validated. Following successful validation, the residual risk should be reassessed and submitted for appropriate risk acceptance or closure.
